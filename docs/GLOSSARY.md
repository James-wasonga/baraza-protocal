# Glossary

**Chama** — a rotating savings and credit group, common across East
Africa. Members contribute regularly; payouts rotate or fund shared
goals.

**ROSCA** — Rotating Savings and Credit Association, the general term
for this pattern worldwide (susu in West Africa, tandas in Latin
America).

**Baraza** — Swahili for a traditional community council used to
resolve local disputes; the protocol's namesake.

**SBT (Soulbound Token)** — a non-transferable on-chain token. Here,
it represents reputation tied permanently to one wallet.

**Commit-reveal** — a two-step randomness scheme: commit to a hidden
value first, reveal it later. Prevents anyone from choosing a
favorable outcome after seeing who's eligible.
